About This Site

Welcome to "Design Patterns in Python"! This site is dedicated to providing a clear, practical learning path for understanding and applying software design patterns using the Python programming language, with specific examples often focusing on the Django web framework.

Our goal is to break down complex concepts into manageable steps, starting from foundational principles like SOLID and moving through creational, structural, and behavioral patterns. We believe that learning patterns is not just about theory, but about seeing them in action and knowing when and how to use them effectively in real-world projects.

Whether you're a beginner looking to strengthen your coding practices or an experienced developer aiming to write more maintainable and scalable code, we hope you find the resources here helpful.

This site is maintained by Alin Morosanu. Feel free to explore the different phases of the learning path and dive into the examples.
